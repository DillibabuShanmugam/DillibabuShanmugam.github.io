I am a PhD candidate at Vernam Lab, Worcester Polytechnic Institute, advised by Prof. Patrick Schaumont. My work spans side-channel analysis, fault injection, ASIC design, and post-quantum cryptography. I am completing my PhD in December 2026 and am open to industry R&D roles in hardware security in the United States. I am a member of IEEE, ACM, and IACR.

Research focus:

  • Bridging the gap between pre-silicon models and post-silicon evaluation of side-channel and fault analysis, using machine-learning techniques.
  • Sane vs. weird machine analysis using graph neural networks (GNN) and SFT/QLoRA fine-tuned LLMs on scan-chain data.
  • Microscaling (MX) format fault analysis using machine learning (CNN/GNN) and scan-chain data, on RISC-V accelerators for Edge-AI.
  • An open-source reference for reproducible side-channel assessment of post-quantum cryptography standards, with protected and unprotected ML-KEM and ML-DSA implementations.
