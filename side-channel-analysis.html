Side-Channel Analysis

My side-channel work asks whether a design leaks its secrets through power, and whether that can be decided before the chip exists. It covers four topics.

Cross-layer side-channel analysis

The same leakage test on pre-silicon models and on measured power traces, to check that the model verdict holds on hardware. More

PQC custom instruction analysis

Per-instruction leakage assessment of a unified ML-KEM-1024 and ML-DSA-44 accelerator with 62 custom instructions (IEEE FPT 2026). More

openPQC reference implementation

Open-source ML-KEM and ML-DSA on a CV32E40X RISC-V core with a CV-X-IF coprocessor, protected and unprotected. More

PRISM PQC accelerators

ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA and HQC accelerators on one TSMC 65 nm RISC-V chip, unprotected and masked on the same die. More
